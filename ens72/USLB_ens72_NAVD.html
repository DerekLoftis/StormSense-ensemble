Bias and errors are forecast minus observed. High waters: every observed high tide in a forecast's period, paired with the forecast's highest water within 3 hours; a high tide is counted once for each run that forecast it. Flood stages are the NWS Action, Minor, Moderate and Major levels: hit = both reached it, miss = the water reached it and the forecast did not, false alarm = the forecast reached it and the water did not.
