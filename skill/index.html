SS-Skill = 0.25[R² + e−|Bias|/σ + e−MAE/σ + e−RMSE/σ], σ the population SD of the observations in the window: 1 is perfect, about 0.45 is no skill. Each value is the mean over the runs verified in the last 10 days, weighted toward the newest (e-folding 3 days); rows are ordered by SS-Skill over each forecast's own period. Bias and errors are forecast minus observed. High waters: every observed high tide in a forecast's period, paired with the forecast's highest water within 3 hours; a high tide is counted once for each run that forecast it. Flood stages are the NWS Action, Minor, Moderate and Major levels: hit = both reached it, miss = the water reached it and the forecast did not, false alarm = the forecast reached it and the water did not.

Per station and lead band (hours after the last observation), the member with the best recent skill S = 0.25[R2 + e^(-|Bias|/sd) + e^(-MAE/sd) + e^(-RMSE/sd)] (mean over 10 days, weighted exp(-age/3 d)); it must lead the current pick by 0.01 for 2 updates running. P-ETSS until 7 days of verified runs exist and the member has 12 scores; Tidewatch, the baseline and the tide alone are never picked; stations whose archived observations were another gauge's (GS01, USAR, USBB, USCB, USNR, USSB, USSC) stay on P-ETSS.
